Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Draft for the owner to review before subscriptions open.

Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time. After you cancel, you will not be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

  • Monthly and yearly plans: if you are not satisfied, email us within 7 days of a payment and we will refund that payment.
  • Weekly plan: email us within 48 hours of a payment for a refund.
  • If you forgot to cancel and were charged for a renewal, contact us within the same period and we will review it fairly.

How to ask

Email Shoaib_h11@yahoo.com from the address you used to sign up, and tell us which payment you mean. We usually reply within 3 working days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, and they can take several days to appear.

If something is wrong

If a paid lesson does not open or does not work as described, tell us. We will fix it or refund you.

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. Its own rules may also apply to disputes and chargebacks.
