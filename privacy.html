Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Draft in plain language. It has not been legally reviewed and does not claim compliance with any particular privacy law.

Stored on your device

Your level, finished lessons, quiz scores, vocabulary progress, streak, goals, voice choice and any private notes are saved in your browser on your device. This lets the app work and remember your progress. Clearing your browser data erases it.

If you sign in with Google

Google shares your name, email address and account ID with us through Firebase Authentication, so we know who you are. Your progress, and any profile details you add (name, country, first language, learning goal, a small picture), are then also saved in Google Firestore under your account so they can sync between devices. Only your own account can read this, and the app owner can see it in the Firebase console.

Premium access and requests

If you ask for Premium access, we save your account ID, name, email address and the time of your request. When access is granted, we save an access record with its end date. We use this only to decide who can open paid lessons.

Payments

When subscriptions open, a third-party payment provider will process payments and may collect your card details, billing details and tax information under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card details.

Feedback form

The Give Feedback button opens a Google Form. What you type there is stored by Google Forms and read by us. Please do not enter passwords or payment details.

Voice and microphone

Listening uses voices on your device. When you use speech recognition, your browser (for example Chrome) may send your voice to its own speech service to turn it into text. SpeakUp English does not record or store audio.

Other services

The app loads a font from Google Fonts, so Google can see that your device requested it. Your browser also saves app files for installing and offline use. We use no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

What we do not collect

Passwords (Google handles sign-in), card numbers, your location, your contacts, or photos other than a picture you choose to add.

Your choices

You can reset your progress in the app, sign out at any time, and clear your browser data. To ask us to delete your account data, or to ask what we hold about you, email Shoaib_h11@yahoo.com.
